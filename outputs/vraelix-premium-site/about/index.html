A note from the founder

A practical idea,
a wider horizon.

Ansh Kumar is the Founder & CEO of VRAELIX.

VRAELIX is being built around a simple principle: good digital work is grounded in business purpose. A website, an automation or a custom tool should have a clear role in the way people discover, understand or operate a business.

That principle is the starting point for every conversation. It keeps the work focused on what matters now while leaving room for what may be useful next.
