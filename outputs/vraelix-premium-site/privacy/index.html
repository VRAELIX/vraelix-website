This page explains the basic terms for this static website. Please read it alongside the information relevant to your use.

Website information

How information is handled

This static website has no VRAELIX-hosted database. When you submit the Request a Quote form, your browser sends the enquiry details to Zapier. VRAELIX has configured Zapier to route those enquiries to Google Sheets for follow-up. The website uses browser storage for the privacy preference and a temporary copy used by the confirmation page. It does not use analytics or advertising pixels.

Information you may enter

The form asks for your name, email address, phone number, requested service, project description, budget, timeline and preferred contact method. Company or business name is optional. Please include only information needed to respond to your enquiry.

Browser storage and confirmation

After the form receives a successful response, the browser keeps a temporary sessionStorage copy of the submitted brief so the Thank You page can show the confirmation details. This copy remains in the current tab session and is not sent again from storage. A separate essential-only localStorage preference remembers your privacy choice and contains no personal information.

Third-party processing

The form sends submitted information to Zapier, which VRAELIX uses to route enquiry records to Google Sheets. Zapier and Google handle information under their own terms and privacy practices. The VRAELIX website does not control those providers’ retention periods or settings. Review Zapier Privacy Policy.

Retention and requests

The website does not set a retention period for enquiry records in Zapier or Google Sheets. VRAELIX should review and manage retention in those connected accounts. To ask about an enquiry or request its deletion, contact vraelix29@gmail.com.

Privacy preference

The privacy preference is stored in browser localStorage so the notice does not appear on every page load. It contains no personal information. You can reopen the notice from the footer at any time.

External contact services

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram or other external services are governed by those providers’ own privacy practices. VRAELIX does not control how those services handle information.
