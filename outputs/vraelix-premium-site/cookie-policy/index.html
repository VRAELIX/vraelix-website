Website information

Cookies and browser storage

The VRAELIX website does not set analytics, advertising or personalisation cookies. It uses essential browser localStorage to remember the privacy preference and sessionStorage to keep a temporary copy of a successfully submitted enquiry for the Thank You page.

Privacy preference

The preference stores only an essential-only setting so the notice does not appear on every page load. It contains no personal information. You can reopen the notice from the footer to review the current settings.

Enquiry submission

SessionStorage itself is not sent to a server. When you submit the form, your browser sends the enquiry details to Zapier, which VRAELIX uses to route the enquiry into Google Sheets. After the form receives a successful response, a temporary copy of the submitted brief remains in the current tab session so the Thank You page can show the confirmation details.

Third-party services

Zapier and Google process submitted enquiry information under their own terms and privacy practices. External links may also use their own cookies or storage when you visit those services. Review Zapier Privacy Policy.

Retention

The website does not set the retention period for enquiry records in Zapier or Google Sheets. VRAELIX should review and manage retention in those connected accounts.
