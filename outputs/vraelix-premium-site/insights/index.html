01 / Planning

What should a business website make clear?

A visitor should be able to understand the offer, recognise whether it fits and find a next step. Start by listing the questions customers ask before they contact you. Then make sure the most useful answers appear where people expect them.

02 / Technology

When is business automation worth exploring?

Look for work that repeats, follows clear rules and moves information between known steps. Map the process first, including exceptions and handoffs. Automation is a good candidate when it makes that flow simpler without removing necessary judgement.

03 / Discovery

How do you scope a digital project with confidence?

Describe the person who needs the change, the friction they face and what would be different if the work succeeds. A clear problem statement gives the team a way to prioritise features, estimate effort and decide what can wait.
